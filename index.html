Helpful guide

6 Signs Your RO Needs a Service

Spot these early and a simple service can save you a costly repair.

Water flow has slowed

Usually clogged pre-filters or a tired membrane. The tank takes longer to fill.

Taste or smell has changed

Old carbon filters stop removing odour. Water may taste flat, salty or off.

Water is leaking

Loose fittings or a cracked housing or pipe. Most fixed quickly if caught early.

Unusual noises

A loud or constant running pump can mean low pressure or a fault in the pump.

Purifier keeps running

If the tank is full but the motor never stops, the auto shut-off may need attention.

Last service was months ago

Most home RO purifiers need a service every 4 to 6 months, even if they look fine.

Helpful guide

6 Signs Your Geyser Needs a Service

Spot these early and a simple service can save you a costly repair.

Slow heating

Mineral scale buildup coats the element, making it take much longer to heat water.

Water leaking

Dripping or pooling water points to loose pipe fittings, worn gaskets, or a failing valve.

Rusty water

Brown or metallic-smelling water means the inner tank or anode rod is corroding

Unusual noises

Hissing, popping, or banging sounds happen when water boils through thick bottom sediment.

Tripping power

A short-circuiting heating element or moisture on internal wiring will trip your home's MCB.

Low water pressure

Settled sediment and scale clog the inlet and outlet pipes, restricting hot water flow.
